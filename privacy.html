Election: Run For President - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Summary

Election: Run For President requires no accounts, usernames, or sign-ups, and has no advertising or analytics. Online Multiplayer sends game data and optional text chat messages between the two players. Chat messages are visible to your opponent, so do not include personal information. See below for how multiplayer and chat work.

Introduction

Welcome to Election: Run For President. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what the game and our website at electionrunforpresident.com do and do not do with information. It applies to the game on iOS, Android, and Windows (Steam), and to the website.

What We Do NOT Collect

We do not ask you to provide personal information to play. Apart from information you choose to include in multiplayer chat, we do NOT collect or share:

  • Personal identification information, such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Accounts, usernames, passwords, or player profiles
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or gameplay statistics
  • Device information or device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or any other data from your device
  • Any other personal or identifying information

Single-Player and Local Play

Single-player games, games against the computer, and local pass-and-play games on one device never send any information over the internet. Everything about these games stays on your device.

Online Multiplayer: A Limited Exception

Online Multiplayer is the only part of the game that communicates over the internet. It is an exception to the "no data leaves your device" statement above, so we want to be completely clear about how it works.

How a multiplayer match works

  • One player creates a match and the game shows a short room code.
  • That player shares the room code with a friend outside of the game, for example by text message, email, or in person. The game has no friend lists or player search. Once connected, the two players can exchange text messages within their match.
  • The second player types in the room code to join the same match.
  • The room code is the only thing that connects the two players. No name or account is required. Each connection is assigned a random, temporary identifier to manage its seat in the match. This identifier is held in memory by the game and is not a persistent device identifier.

What Online Multiplayer does NOT include

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins
  • No usernames, display names, or player profiles
  • No public matchmaking. You cannot be matched with strangers. You can only play with someone you have personally given a room code to.
  • No audio or voice chat, and no access to your microphone
  • No video, camera access, or screen sharing
  • No friend lists or contact lists
  • No leaderboards or public player rankings

In-game text chat

Online Multiplayer includes optional text chat with your opponent. Messages are limited to 140 characters. An automatic filter checks for blocked language and common patterns for contact details and links, but it cannot catch everything. Do not share personal information in chat. Messages are sent through the multiplayer connection service to the other player in your match.

What information is sent

Online Multiplayer sends the information needed to keep the game state in sync between the two players, such as the candidates chosen, the cards played, state leanings, campaign funds, and whose turn it is. If you use text chat, your message text and player seat (Player 1 or Player 2) are also sent to your opponent.

What we do NOT do with multiplayer information

  • There are no accounts, usernames, or player profiles. You are never asked to provide a name or email address.
  • We do not collect or record device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location.
  • We do not use multiplayer data for analytics, advertising, profiling, or tracking.
  • We do not sell multiplayer data. Our multiplayer connection service carries game data and chat messages to your opponent.
  • We do not link a room code to a person. A room code is a temporary meeting point for a single match and is not an identity.

Temporary match data and retention

Supabase processes multiplayer traffic for us. Temporary room records include the room code, connection identifiers, selected game settings, readiness, and match state needed to run and reconnect to the match. Finished rooms are deleted when the host finishes, with a backup cleanup for finished records older than 15 minutes. A scheduled cleanup also deletes inactive playing rooms after one hour and inactive waiting, lobby, or abandoned rooms after 30 minutes; deletion occurs on the next scheduled cleanup. We do not maintain a history of players or completed matches. Chat messages are relayed to your opponent through the live connection and are not written to our room database. The game does not save chat history to load in later matches. Your opponent may read or copy messages you send.

Internet infrastructure

Like any internet connection, the network services that carry multiplayer traffic must momentarily handle technical connection details, such as an IP address, in order to deliver data between the two players. We do not use this information for advertising or profiling. Infrastructure providers may process technical logs to operate and secure their services; these are subject to the providers’ retention practices.

Game Data Storage

Game achievements and in-progress single-player games are stored locally on your device only. This data does not leave your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

Our game does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not sell information. Online Multiplayer uses Supabase to store temporary room records and relay game-state data and optional chat messages between the two players, as described above. Supabase’s privacy information is available at https://supabase.com/privacy. The platform stores you download from (Apple App Store, Google Play, and Steam) have their own privacy policies that apply to your use of those stores.

Children's Privacy

Our game is suitable for all ages. We do not ask anyone, including children, to provide personal information to play. Online Multiplayer requires no account, name, or personal details, and a child can only play with someone to whom a room code has been given outside of the game. Parents should be aware of who their child shares a room code with and that players in a match can exchange text messages. The automatic chat filter cannot catch every inappropriate message or personal detail. Children should not share personal information in chat.

Data Security

Local game data remains on your device. Multiplayer game data and chat messages travel through the connection service to the other player. Avoid including personal information in chat, since your opponent can read the messages you send.

Your Rights

There is no player account or saved chat history to access, correct, or delete from within the game. You can delete the game from your device at any time, which removes all locally stored game data. This does not remove messages your opponent has already seen or copied.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
